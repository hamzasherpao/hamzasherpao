# Hi, I'm Muhammad Hamza 👋

Ph.D. candidate at **Peking University** working at the intersection of **CFD/FSI simulation** and **embedded sensing** for soft bodies in flow. Before my Ph.D., I spent 3+ years as a CFD/CAE engineer in aerospace, renewable energy, and fluid machinery.

### What I work on

- **Simulation and design optimization:** CFD and fluid-structure interaction (FSI), aerodynamic and hydrodynamic shape optimization, surrogate-based design
- **Embedded sensing and localization:** piezoelectric (PVDF) sensing in soft bodies, signal-conditioning hardware, synchronized data acquisition, and machine-learning estimation of body shape, flow conditions, and position, deployed in real time
- **Flow-energy harvesting:** bio-inspired flexible hydrofoils, from optimization to water-channel and field tests

### Tools

ANSYS Fluent · ANSYS ICEM · Star-CCM+ · COMSOL Multiphysics · OpenFOAM · IBAMR · Femap/Nastran · MATLAB · Python · C++
